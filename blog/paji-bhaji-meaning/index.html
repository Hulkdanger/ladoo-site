Paji or bhaji? What to call an older brother or sister in Punjabi

By the Ladoo team · Published November 13, 2026 · Updated November 13, 2026

Paji and bhaji are the same word, ਭਾਜੀ. It means "respected brother," and it is what you call an older brother, an older cousin, or any man around your age or a bit older. An older sister is ਭੈਣ ਜੀ (bhain ji). In most Punjabi families you do not call an older sibling by their bare name.

Why it is spelled both ways

ਭਾਜੀ is ਭਾ (bha), a short form of the word for brother, plus ਜੀ (ji), the respect word. The spelling says bh. The mouth says something else. At the start of a Punjabi word, ਭ is pronounced as a plain p, and the vowel after it drops to a low tone. So ਭਾਜੀ comes out as "paji" with the pitch dipping on the first syllable.

People who spell by the letter write bhaji. People who spell by ear write paji or paaji. Praji is the same idea built on ਭਰਾ (bhra), the full word for brother, which sounds like "pra." In Shahmukhi the word is بھا جی. The sound rule is in our pronunciation guide, with audio.

Two other words get mixed up with it. Hindi has an unrelated word पाजी (paji) that means a scoundrel, so a Hindi dictionary gives a rude answer. And ਭਾਜੀ in Punjabi is also a cooked vegetable dish, and the boxes of sweets a family sends out before a wedding.

The other words for brother

ਭਰਾ (bhra) is the noun. You use it to talk about a brother: ਇਹ ਮੇਰਾ ਭਰਾ ਹੈ (eh mera bhra hai), "this is my brother." You do not call him that to his face.

ਵੀਰ (veer) and ਵੀਰ ਜੀ (veer ji) are warmer. Veer is what sisters tend to say, and it is the word in the songs sung at Rakhri.

ਭਾਈ (bhai) means brother too, but in Punjabi it is mostly a title. Sikhs use it for respected men in their history, like Bhai Mardana, who travelled with Guru Nanak, and for the granthi at the gurdwara, who is Bhai Sahib.

ਬਾਈ (bai) is what you hear in the Malwa region. ਬਾਈ is also the word for twenty-two, so people text it as "22 ji."

Older sisters

ਭੈਣ (bhain) is the noun, and ਭੈਣ ਜੀ (bhain ji) is what you call her. The same sound rule applies, so it comes out as "pain ji," and you will see it written bhenji or penji. ਦੀਦੀ (didi) came in from Hindi and plenty of families use it.

Families who write Punjabi in Shahmukhi often say آپا (aapa) or باجی (baji) for an older sister. Baji is a sister and bhaji is a brother, one letter apart in English.

Younger siblings get their name

Titles only go upward. A younger brother or sister is called by name, or by a pet name. When you talk about them, add ਛੋਟਾ (chhota, small) or ਵੱਡਾ (vadda, big). ਮੇਰਾ ਛੋਟਾ ਭਰਾ (mera chhota bhra) is "my younger brother," and ਮੇਰੀ ਵੱਡੀ ਭੈਣ (meri vaddi bhain) is "my older sister."

The people they marry have their own words. An older brother's wife is ਭਾਬੀ (bhabi), and a sister's husband is ਜੀਜਾ ਜੀ (jija ji). The rest of the tree is in Punjabi family words.

People who are not your brother

Paji and bhain ji are also how you speak to strangers. In one of the stories in Ladoo, a mother at a vegetable stall asks, ਭਾਜੀ, ਟਮਾਟਰ ਕਿਵੇਂ ਲਾਏ? (bhaji, tamatar kiven laye?), "Brother, how much are the tomatoes?" The seller is not her brother. He is a man about her age, and that is enough.

Roughly: someone your age or a bit older is paji or bhain ji. Someone your parents' age is uncle ji or aunty ji. Someone your grandparents' age is ਬਾਬਾ ਜੀ (baba ji) or ਮਾਤਾ ਜੀ (mata ji).

All of these go with ਤੁਸੀਂ (tusi), the polite "you," and the verbs that match it. That part is in tusi or tu.

Common questions

What does paji mean in Punjabi?

Paji (ਭਾਜੀ) means "respected brother." It is said to an older brother, an older male cousin, or any man around your age or older, as a sign of respect. It is also spelled bhaji and paaji.

Is it paji or bhaji?

Both spell the same word. It is written ਭਾਜੀ with the letter for bh, but at the start of a word Punjabi pronounces that letter as p with a low tone, so it sounds like paji.

What do you call an older sister in Punjabi?

ਭੈਣ ਜੀ (bhain ji), also written bhenji. Many families say didi, and some say aapa or baji.

What does veer ji mean?

Veer ji (ਵੀਰ ਜੀ) means "dear brother." It is a warm way to address a brother, and sisters use it most.

Can you call a stranger paji?

Yes. Paji is the normal polite way to address a shopkeeper, a driver, or any man about your age or a little older. For a man your parents' age, say uncle ji.
