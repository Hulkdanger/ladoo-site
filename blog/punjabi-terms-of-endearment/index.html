Punjabi terms of endearment: jaan, sohneya, heeriye, and what they mean

By the Ladoo team · Published November 25, 2026 · Updated November 25, 2026

The most common Punjabi word for someone you love is ਜਾਨ (jaan), "my life." A man gets called ਸੋਹਣਿਆ (sohneya), "handsome one," and a woman ਸੋਹਣੀਏ (sohniye), "beautiful one." Parents and grandparents say ਪੁੱਤ (putt) to every child, daughters included.

Words for a partner

  • ਜਾਨ (jaan) means life. It works for a man or a woman, and it is the one most couples use. In Shahmukhi it is جان.
  • ਸੋਹਣਿਆ (sohneya) is said to a man and ਸੋਹਣੀਏ (sohniye) to a woman. Both come from sohna, good-looking.
  • ਮਾਹੀ (mahi) is a beloved man. The word first meant a buffalo herder. In the folk tale of Sohni and Mahiwal, the hero takes a job herding buffalo to stay near Sohni, and the job turned into a word for the man you love.
  • ਚੰਨ (chann) means moon, چن in Shahmukhi. ਚੰਨ ਮਾਹੀ (chann mahi) puts the two together.
  • ਹੀਰੀਏ (heeriye) means "my Heer." Heer is the heroine of the love story Heer Ranjha. ਰਾਂਝਣਾ (ranjhna) is the matching word for a man.
  • ਸੱਜਣਾ (sajjna) and ਢੋਲਾ (dhola) are older words for a beloved. You will hear them in folk songs more than at home.

Why so many of them end in -eya and -iye

Punjabi changes the end of a word when you call out to someone. A masculine word takes -a or -eya, and a feminine word takes -e or -iye. Sohna becomes sohneya and sohni becomes sohniye. ਕੁੜੀ (kudi, girl) becomes ਕੁੜੀਏ (kudiye). ਮੁੰਡਾ (munda, boy) becomes ਮੁੰਡਿਆ (mundeya). ਯਾਰ (yaar, friend) becomes ਯਾਰਾ (yaara), and ਚੰਨ becomes ਚੰਨਾ (channa). Heer becomes heeriye.

That ending means "hey, you." It is why Punjabi song titles look the way they do: Heeriye, Soniye, Yaara.

Words for children

Elders use these on anyone younger, grown adults included.

  • ਪੁੱਤ (putt) or ਪੁੱਤਰ (puttar) means son, and daughters get called it just as often.
  • ਬੱਚੇ (bachche) means child. ਬੱਚੇ, ਰੋਟੀ ਖਾ ਲੈ (bachche, roti kha lai) is "eat your food, child," said to a forty-year-old.
  • ਰਾਜਾ (raja, king) and ਰਾਣੀ (rani, queen) go to small children.
  • ਸ਼ੇਰ (sher) means lion. ਮੇਰਾ ਸ਼ੇਰ ਪੁੱਤ (mera sher putt), "my lion of a son," is what you hear after a good report card.
  • ਲਾਡੋ (lado) is for a daughter who is a little spoiled. It comes from ਲਾਡ (laad), pampering.
  • ਲੱਡੂ (laddu) is the round sweet, and a round-cheeked baby gets called one. This app is named after the same sweet.

I love you, I miss you, I like you

"I love you" is ਮੈਂ ਤੈਨੂੰ ਪਿਆਰ ਕਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ (main tainu piar karda haan) if a man says it, and ਮੈਂ ਤੈਨੂੰ ਪਿਆਰ ਕਰਦੀ ਹਾਂ (main tainu piar kardi haan) if a woman says it. The verb follows the speaker. Saying it to a boy or to a girl does not change a word. In Shahmukhi it is میں تینوں پیار کردا ہاں, or کردی ہاں from a woman. The audio is on I love you in Punjabi.

"I miss you" is ਮੈਨੂੰ ਤੇਰੀ ਯਾਦ ਆਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ (mainu teri yaad aundi hai). Word for word it says "your memory comes to me," and it is the same whoever says it.

"I like you" runs the other way. ਤੂੰ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਚੰਗਾ ਲੱਗਦਾ ਹੈਂ (tu mainu changa lagda hain) is said to a man, and ਤੂੰ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਚੰਗੀ ਲੱਗਦੀ ਹੈਂ (tu mainu changi lagdi hain) is said to a woman. Here the person you like is the subject of the sentence, so the verb follows them.

All three use ਤੂੰ (tu), the close form of "you." Many couples say ਤੁਸੀਂ (tusi) to each other, and then tainu becomes ਤੁਹਾਨੂੰ (tuhanu) and teri becomes ਤੁਹਾਡੀ (tuhadi). How that choice works is in tusi or tu.

Song words that are not pet names

Four words turn up in a lot of Punjabi choruses. They describe a person, and nobody uses them as a pet name.

  • ਗੱਭਰੂ (gabru) is a young man in his prime, strong and a bit of a show-off.
  • ਮੁਟਿਆਰ (mutiyar) is a young woman, the counterpart of gabru.
  • ਪਟੋਲਾ (patola) is a rag doll. In songs it means a woman dressed to be looked at.
  • ਬਿੱਲੋ (billo) comes from ਬਿੱਲੀ (billi), cat. It is a girl with light, cat-like eyes.

Who can say what

Jaan, sohneya, and the other partner words stay between couples, and most couples keep them out of earshot of elders. Putt and bachche only go down in age: your nani says it to you, and you do not say it back. With anyone older, respect does the job of affection. You add ji and use the right family word, which is covered in Punjabi family words and paji or bhaji.

Common questions

What do Punjabi couples call each other?

Jaan (ਜਾਨ), meaning life, is the most common. Men are called sohneya (ਸੋਹਣਿਆ) or mahi (ਮਾਹੀ), and women are called sohniye (ਸੋਹਣੀਏ) or heeriye (ਹੀਰੀਏ).

How do you say I love you in Punjabi to a girl or to a boy?

The sentence is the same for both. A man says main tainu piar karda haan (ਮੈਂ ਤੈਨੂੰ ਪਿਆਰ ਕਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ) and a woman says main tainu piar kardi haan (ਮੈਂ ਤੈਨੂੰ ਪਿਆਰ ਕਰਦੀ ਹਾਂ). The verb changes with who is speaking. Who hears it makes no difference.

How do you say I miss you in Punjabi?

Mainu teri yaad aundi hai (ਮੈਨੂੰ ਤੇਰੀ ਯਾਦ ਆਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ). It means "your memory comes to me," and it is the same for male and female speakers.

What does heeriye mean?

Heeriye (ਹੀਰੀਏ) means "my Heer." Heer is the heroine of the Punjabi love story Heer Ranjha, so calling a woman heeriye calls her your great love.

What does puttar mean?

Puttar (ਪੁੱਤਰ) means son. Punjabi elders say it, or the short form putt, to any child or younger person, daughters included.

What does patola mean in Punjabi?

Patola (ਪਟੋਲਾ) is a rag doll. In Punjabi songs it is slang for a well-dressed, good-looking woman.
