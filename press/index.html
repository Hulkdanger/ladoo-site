The one-liner

Ladoo is the most complete app for speaking Punjabi and reading Gurmukhi, built for the diaspora.

Facts

  • Ladoo teaches spoken Punjabi and the Gurmukhi script in two separate tracks, so heritage speakers can skip straight to what they are missing.
  • 170+ bite-size lessons, and every one of them is free. No lesson is ever locked.
  • The Gurmukhi track covers all 35 letters plus vowel signs and numerals, with native audio and finger tracing on every letter.
  • Bhaji, the AI tutor, holds spoken Punjabi conversations, remembers a learner's mistakes, and drills them.
  • Eight illustrated Punjabi stories, narrated line by line.
  • Ladoo Pro is $9.99 a month or $69.99 a year with a 7-day free trial; details on the pricing page.
  • 4,000+ downloads on iOS and Android.
  • Gurdwara and school Punjabi classes get Pro free through the education program.
  • Made by Ladoo Learn Punjabi LLC, based in California. More on the about page.
