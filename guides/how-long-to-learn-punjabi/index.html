How long does it take to learn Punjabi?

By the Ladoo team · Published August 31, 2026

With 10 to 15 minutes a day: two to three weeks to read basic Gurmukhi, two to three months to hold simple conversations, and six months to a year to feel comfortable across everyday topics. Heritage speakers who already understand Punjabi cut those numbers sharply. Everything below is the detail behind those estimates, including what actually moves them.

The milestones

Reading Gurmukhi: two to three weeks. The script is phonetic. There are 35 core letters plus vowel signs and numerals, each letter maps to one sound, and there are no silent letters to memorize. Learners who do a few letters a day with audio are sounding out real words inside a month. The alphabet chart is the fastest place to start.

First conversations: two to three months. Greetings, ordering food, asking about family, understanding answers. This milestone depends almost entirely on whether you practice out loud. Tapping matching exercises does not build it; saying sentences and getting corrected does.

Comfortable everyday Punjabi: six months to a year. Following group conversation, joking, disagreeing, switching topics. This is where consistency beats intensity: 15 daily minutes for a year beats weekend cramming every time.

For scale: the US Foreign Service Institute estimates about 1,100 classroom hours for English speakers to reach professional working proficiency in Hindi and Urdu, Punjabi's closest widely taught relatives. That is the ceiling for full professional fluency, not the bar for talking with your family, which arrives far earlier.

What actually changes the timeline

Heritage exposure is the biggest variable by far. If you grew up hearing Punjabi, your listening comprehension and accent are already built, and you are converting a passive vocabulary instead of acquiring one. Many heritage speakers hold their first real conversations within weeks of starting deliberate practice; our heritage speaker guide covers why.

The second variable is speaking time. Minutes spent producing Punjabi out loud predict progress better than total study time. The third is streak length: daily contact keeps words retrievable, and gaps quietly refund your progress. Short daily lessons exist precisely because showing up is the whole game.

Tones deserve one honest warning. Punjabi is one of the few tonal Indo-Aryan languages, and the difference between ਘਰ and ਕਰ is carried by pitch. You cannot read your way to hearing it; you need audio from the first week, which is why every word in Ladoo ships with native-quality sound.

Common questions

How long does it take to learn Gurmukhi?

Two to three weeks of short daily practice for most learners. Gurmukhi is phonetic, with 35 core letters plus vowel signs, and each letter maps to one sound, so once you know the shapes you can sound out real words immediately.

Can I learn Punjabi in 3 months?

In three months of daily practice you can realistically read Gurmukhi, handle greetings and everyday requests, and hold simple conversations about family, food, and daily life. You will not be fluent, and anyone promising fluency in three months is selling something. Heritage speakers who already understand Punjabi move faster, often speaking in weeks.

Is Punjabi harder than Hindi to learn?

They are close relatives with similar grammar, so the overall effort is comparable. Punjabi adds tones, which Hindi does not have, and that takes listening practice. In exchange, Gurmukhi is one of the most regular scripts in South Asia, so reading comes faster than most learners expect.
