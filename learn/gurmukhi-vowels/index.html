Gurmukhi vowels: mukta and the nine laga matra

By the Ladoo team · Published August 31, 2026

Gurmukhi has ten vowel sounds. One of them, mukta, is invisible: every consonant carries a built-in short "a" unless a vowel sign changes it, so ਕ alone already says "ka". The other nine are written with signs called laga matra that attach above, below, or beside the consonant. Learn these nine shapes and the invisible tenth, and you can voice any Punjabi word you see.

The nine vowel signs

Each sign is shown on its own here; on the page it always rides a consonant. Tap play to hear its name.

ਾ ਕੰਨਾ (kanna) aː

A vertical line AFTER a letter that lengthens its vowel to a long 'aa'. With Aira it forms ਆ.

ਿ ਸਿਹਾਰੀ (sihari) ɪ

WRITTEN BEFORE the letter but READ AFTER it, a common surprise. With Iri it forms ਇ.

ੀ ਬਿਹਾਰੀ (bihari) iː

Written after the letter, it gives a long 'ee'. With Iri it forms ਈ.

ੁ ਔਂਕੜ (aunkar) ʊ

A small mark UNDER the letter for a short 'u'. With Ura it forms ਉ.

ੂ ਦੁਲੈਂਕੜ (dulainkar) uː

A mark under the letter for a long 'oo'. With Ura it forms ਊ.

ੇ ਲਾਂਵ (lavan) eː

A stroke above the letter for a long 'e'. With Iri it forms ਏ.

ੈ ਦੁਲਾਂਵ (dulavan) ɛː

Two strokes above for an open 'ai'. With Aira it forms ਐ.

ੋ ਹੋੜਾ (hora) oː

A mark above the letter for a long 'o'. With Ura it forms ਓ.

ੌ ਕਨੌੜਾ (kanaura) ɔː

Two marks above for an open 'au'. With Aira it forms ਔ.

The three bearer letters

A vowel at the start of a word has no consonant to lean on, so Gurmukhi gives it one of three bearer letters: ura, aira, and iri. Which bearer pairs with which vowel sign is fixed, and it is the first thing the script track drills, because it is the one genuinely arbitrary part of an otherwise regular system.

ੳ ਊੜਾ (ura) (vowel carrier u/o/oo)

Ura makes no sound on its own. It is a 'chair' that carries the vowels u, oo and o (ਉ ਊ ਓ).

ਅ ਐੜਾ (aira) ə (schwa)

Aira is the only bearer with its own sound: a short 'uh'. Every consonant also hides this sound unless a vowel sign changes it.

ੲ ਈੜੀ (iri) (vowel carrier i/ee/e)

Iri makes no sound alone. It carries the vowels i, ee and e (ਇ ਈ ਏ).

Nasal marks and the doubler

Two marks send a vowel through the nose, tippi for the short vowels and bindi for the open ones, and addak doubles the consonant that follows it. These three do heavy work: ਪੰਜ (panj, five) needs its tippi and ਇੱਕ (ikk, one) needs its addak.

ੰ ਟਿੱਪੀ (tippi) ◌̃ (nasal)

A small mark that nasalizes the vowel by sending air through the nose. Used over short/most vowels.

ਂ ਬਿੰਦੀ (bindi) ◌̃ (nasal)

A dot that nasalizes the vowel; used over the open vowels (kanna, lavan, etc.).

ੱ ਅੱਧਕ (addak) ː (doubling)

Doubles the NEXT consonant. Hold it a beat longer. ਪੱਕਾ is 'pakkaa', not 'pakaa'.

How to practice them

Read real words early. Take a word you know by ear, ਪਾਣੀ (pani, water) say, and watch the signs do their job: kanna stretches pa, bihari closes it with ee.
