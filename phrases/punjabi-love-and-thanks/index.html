How to say I love you, thank you, and congratulations in Punjabi

By the Ladoo team · Published August 24, 2026

"I love you" in Punjabi is ਮੈਂ ਤੈਨੂੰ ਪਿਆਰ ਕਰਦਾ ਹਾਂ (main tainu piar karda haan) from a male speaker, and ਮੈਂ ਤੈਨੂੰ ਪਿਆਰ ਕਰਦੀ ਹਾਂ (main tainu piar kardi haan) from a female speaker; the verb agrees with whoever is doing the loving. Below are the words of affection and gratitude, each with native audio.

The words

ਪਿਆਰ piār love

The word inside 'I love you': main tainu piar kardā hāṃ (male speaker) or kardī hāṃ (female speaker).

ਧੰਨਵਾਦ dhannvād thank you
ਸ਼ੁਕਰੀਆ shukrīā thanks

Warmer and more everyday than dhannvad.

ਸ਼ੁਕਰ shukar gratitude / thankfulness

As in shukar hai, thank goodness.

ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ khush happy
ਵਧਾਈਆਂ vadhāīāṃ congratulations

What you shout at weddings, births, and new jobs: lakh lakh vadhaiaan, a hundred thousand congratulations.

ਸੋਹਣਾ sohṇā handsome / lovely (m)
ਸੋਹਣੀ sohṇī beautiful (f)
ਸੁੰਦਰ sundar beautiful

Saying thank you without saying thank you

Here is the cultural catch: constant thank-yous can feel oddly distant in Punjabi. Inside the family, gratitude is often shown rather than said, and thanking your mother for dinner every night would earn you a strange look. Dhannvad and shukriya are real and used, with strangers, for favors, in formal settings. With people close to you, warmth does the work: praise the food, ask for seconds, say tusi bahut sohne ho.

How to say sorry

The direct apology is ਮਾਫ਼ ਕਰਨਾ (maaf karna), "forgive me", softened to maaf karna ji with elders. For small bumps and interruptions, English "sorry" itself is completely standard in modern spoken Punjabi.
