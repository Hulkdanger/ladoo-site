The Ladoo Education Program

A discounted first year of Ladoo Pro, through the schools and community organizations that keep Punjabi alive.

What it is

Participating organizations each get a code. Anyone who redeems that code in the app pays $54.99 for their first year of Ladoo Pro (normally $69.99/year). After the first year, the subscription renews at the regular price, and you can cancel any time.

How to redeem

  1. Download Ladoo.

    Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

  2. Create your free account.

  3. Open Profile and tap “Redeem code.”

  4. Enter the code your organization shared.

  5. Pick the yearly plan. The discounted price is already applied.

Redeem the code before you subscribe. Already subscribed at full price? Email us and we will sort it out.

Participating organizations

  • The program is just starting. The first participating organizations will be listed here.

For organizations: how to join

  • Any school, community centre, or cultural organization that teaches or supports Punjabi can join.
  • We verify each one personally.
  • You get a code and a printable poster to share with your community.
  • It is free for the organization, and there is no revenue share.

Common questions

How long does the discount last?

The discount applies to your first year. After that, the subscription renews at the regular price, and you can cancel any time.

Who can use the code?

The code is meant for the community of the organization that shares it. Codes can be retired at any time.

Does the organization pay or earn anything?

No. It is free for the organization, and there is no revenue share either way.

I'm already subscribed at full price. Can I still get the discount?

Email us and we will sort it out.
