Ladoo pricing

By the Ladoo team · Published August 31, 2026

Ladoo is completely free to start, and every lesson is free: all 170+ lessons across the Language track and the Gurmukhi track, with no lesson ever locked. Ladoo Pro is an optional subscription for $9.99 a month or $69.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial.

What you get for free

The free tier is the whole course, not a sample. Every lesson in both tracks is free, from your first greeting to full sentences, and so are the speaking exercises inside them, with word-by-word feedback on your pronunciation. You also get word-card flashcards each day, daily chat with Bhaji, the AI tutor, and the beginner stories. Further stories open one at a time with gems you earn by finishing lessons.

One reviewer put it better than we could: "the free tier is amazing, I haven't felt like it's limited at all."

What Ladoo Pro adds

Pro removes the daily limits and opens the extras. You get unlimited chat and speaking practice with Bhaji, unlimited word cards, all eight stories from day one, the full Gurmukhi writing hub with practice flashcards, an explanation of why a wrong answer was wrong on every question, and a weekly streak rescue.

Pro is $9.99 a month or $69.99 a year. The yearly plan works out to $5.83 a month.

How the free trial works

The trial runs 7 days and nothing is charged on the day you start it. The app reminds you 24 hours before the trial converts, in the app, by notification, and by email, so forgetting to cancel does not cost you money. Cancel anytime from your App Store or Google Play subscription settings and you keep Pro until the period ends.
