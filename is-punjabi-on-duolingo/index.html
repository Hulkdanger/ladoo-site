Is Punjabi on Duolingo?

By the Ladoo team · Published August 7, 2026 · Updated August 7, 2026

No. As of August 2026, Duolingo does not offer a Punjabi course in either direction: you cannot learn Punjabi on Duolingo, and there is no course for Punjabi speakers learning English. Duolingo's community course incubator, which was once the path for new languages like Punjabi, closed in 2021, so there is no course in development either.

Why doesn't Duolingo have Punjabi?

Punjabi has well over 100 million speakers worldwide, more than many languages Duolingo does offer. But Duolingo builds courses in-house now, prioritized by expected subscriber demand, and South Asian languages have never been a focus: Hindi is its only Indian language with a full course. Punjabi adds two extra hurdles: it is written in two scripts (Gurmukhi in India, Shahmukhi in Pakistan), and it is tonal, which makes generic text-to-speech pipelines produce misleading audio.

What to use instead

Here is an honest map of the options, including where each one is strong. We build one of these apps, so weigh that as you read.

  • Ladoo (that's us) teaches only Punjabi: 170+ bite-size lessons, a dedicated Gurmukhi script track with tracing and audio for every letter, 8 narrated stories, and an AI speaking tutor. It is built for heritage and diaspora learners who grew up hearing Punjabi. It is iOS and Android only, and the lesson library is smaller than Ling's today.
  • Ling has the largest Punjabi exercise volume of the Duolingo-style apps and a familiar gamified feel. Its Punjabi content shares a template with 60+ other languages, so cultural context and script depth are thinner.
  • Mango Languages is strong on grammar notes and is often free through public libraries. Check your library card before paying for anything.
  • Pimsleur is audio-first and good for pronunciation and listening during a commute, but it does not teach the Gurmukhi script.
  • uTalk and Simply Learn Punjabi are phrasebook-style apps: useful for travel phrases, not for building grammar or reading.
  • A tutor on a marketplace like Preply or italki remains the fastest route to real conversation, and pairs well with any app on this list.

If your goal is reading Gurmukhi

Start with the script itself rather than a phrase app. Gurmukhi is phonetic and has 35 core letters, so most learners can decode simple words within a few weeks of steady practice. Our free Gurmukhi alphabet chart covers every letter, vowel sign, and numeral with audio, and the Ladoo app turns that chart into a full lesson track with finger tracing.

Will Duolingo ever add Punjabi?

Nothing suggests it is coming. Duolingo has not announced a Punjabi course, the incubator that crowdsourced new languages is gone, and its recent additions have favored large-subscriber markets. If it ships one day, that would be good news for Punjabi overall. Until then, the tools above are the realistic options.
